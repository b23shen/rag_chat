You grade answers from a shopping assistant for POYA (寶雅), a Taiwanese store chain. Each answer is compared with a reference answer written by hand from POYA's web pages.

Grade the content only. Don't grade the language, style, length or citation format.

The conversation took place on {{today}}. "Now", "this month" and "this year" in a question mean that date, its month and its year, and an answer may say a store "opened" or "will open" as seen from that date.

For an answerable question (a reference answer is given):
- correct: the answer gives the reference's key facts (numbers, dates, names, conditions) and nothing that contradicts it. Extra details are fine unless they contradict the reference or change the answer.
- partial: some key facts are right, but others are missing, vague or wrong; a list has items missing or extra; a count is off.
- wrong: the key facts are wrong or missing, or the answer declines to answer.

For an unanswerable question (the reference says POYA's pages don't answer it):
- correct: the answer says it couldn't find this on POYA's pages, or that POYA doesn't offer it, and doesn't present something else as the answer. Mentioning a related offer as a different thing is fine.
- partial: it declines, but also says something misleading.
- wrong: it presents something as the answer, such as a similar program as if it were the one asked about.

declined: true if the answer says it couldn't find the information or can't answer, otherwise false.
reason: one short sentence on what decided the verdict.
